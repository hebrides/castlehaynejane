The Deal

Your skills for a free place to stay. Simple as that.

We're a registered WWOOF USA host (World Wide Opportunities on Organic Farms). The arrangement is the same one WWOOF has run successfully for 50 years: volunteers contribute meaningful work hours in exchange for accommodation and meals on the farm. No money changes hands.

What makes us different: we take skill-matching seriously. We've been burned by "learning experiences" that turned into mutual frustration. We want to know what you're actually good at and what you actually enjoy — and we'll find the overlap with what we genuinely need done. That way you leave having done real work and feeling like you mattered.

What You Get

A place to stay and real work to show for it.

  • Free accommodation on the farm (tent camping or available barn space)
  • Meals provided during working days
  • A genuine skill match — we won't waste your time on work you hate
  • Access to farm trails, river access, and the whole 26 acres
  • An honest reference from Jane if you want one
  • Good stories and at least one cat encounter

Typical stays run one to three weeks. Longer arrangements can be discussed for volunteers with specialized skills or ongoing projects.

Seasonal Positions

Housing-for-skills arrangements — for students, professionals, and career changers.

Beyond the standard week-long WWOOF stay, we're looking for people who want a real season on the farm — four to twelve weeks, deep enough to complete substantial projects. If you're an agriculture or horticulture student who needs supervised field hours, a retired tradesperson who still wants to build things, or a professional in between chapters, we can put your time to genuine use.

The arrangement is the same as WWOOF — housing and meals in exchange for skilled work — but the scope is bigger. A seasonal position means we can take on a vineyard build-out, a full greenhouse operation, a campground opening, or a live event series. You leave with real work completed and a genuine reference from the farm.

Currently looking for:

  • Agriculture or horticulture students seeking supervised field hours or academic credit
  • Retired or semi-retired carpenters, electricians, and plumbers
  • Experienced vineyard or orchard workers (Scuppernong grapes, figs, peaches, brambles, blueberries)
  • Cooks or food service professionals interested in farm kitchen or bar development
  • Event and live music production experience (outdoor festivals and concerts)
  • Permaculture, landscape design, or land management background
