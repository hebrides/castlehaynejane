    Why This Is Different

    This is a for-profit Agritourism startup — not a charity.

    Castle Hayne Jane's Family Farm is structured as an LLC. The Farm Start Up Fundraiser is raising startup capital to build the infrastructure needed to open — and the business has a clear path to revenue. The two biggest near-term channels are an RV campground along the Cape Fear River ($194K/yr projected) and hosted events and festivals ($176K/yr projected). After that: Scuppernong wine grapes, figs, peaches, blueberries and brambles, greenhouse produce, and nursery sales build long-term depth. Total projected annual revenue when fully operational: ~$748,000.

    The market conditions are real. U.S. organic food sales topped $67.6 billion in 2022 and keep climbing. Our location — in the Wilmington metro, surrounded by beach tourism, a major university, a retirement wave, and a nationally recognized restaurant scene — gives us a built-in audience that's actively seeking exactly what we're building.

    The team already has the credentials. Jane is an AirBnB Superhost managing five units on this property right now. Our operations lead spent time at a California organic winery and agritourism destination before bringing that model here. Our agricultural advisor holds an organic certification credential from CCOF (the nation's largest certifier) and studied ecological horticulture at UC Santa Cruz. We've hosted WWOOF volunteers on the farm through the build-out. This is not a concept — it's a company that's already running.

    Your investment funds the bridge: the barns, the stages, the campsite clearing, the orchards. Infrastructure that converts acreage into revenue. These are pre-revenue prices that will not exist once the farm is open.
